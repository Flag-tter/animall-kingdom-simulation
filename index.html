All 💀 Extinctions 🌦️ Weather 📈 Booms/Crashes 📌 Pinned 🦠 Disease 🧭 Migration
Tip: too many carnivores will starve without enough herbivores; too few trees will starve the herbivores. Wolves hunt foxes, so a fox boom won't last forever. Winter slows growth and reproduction; spring speeds it back up. Droughts shrink water holes and storms can strike trees. Darker patches of ground grow trees faster. Rabbits cluster into herds and foxes hunt in small packs; every offspring inherits a slightly mutated speed and size from its parent, so watch the averages drift over generations; and animals remember the last spot they found food, water, or prey instead of searching blind. Dead animals leave a carcass — 🐜 decomposers eat them (or they slowly rot away on their own) and enrich the soil, helping nearby trees grow. Diseases can break out and spread between crowded animals of the same kind. Animals that linger near the edge sometimes wander off and may wander back in later. Foxes and wolves keep loose home ranges — crowding too many dens together adds stress and cuts into reproduction. Use 🔍 Inspect to click any individual, then 📷 to have the camera follow it or 📌 to pin & name it so its whole lineage stays trackable below. Filter the event log by category or species, and hover a death entry to see why it happened.
