Tip: too many carnivores will starve without enough herbivores; too few trees will starve the herbivores. Wolves hunt foxes, so a fox boom won't last forever. Winter slows growth and reproduction; spring speeds it back up. Droughts shrink water holes and storms can strike trees. Darker patches of ground grow trees faster. Rabbits cluster into herds and foxes hunt in small packs; every offspring inherits a slightly mutated speed and size from its parent, so watch the averages drift over generations; and animals remember the last spot they found food, water, or prey instead of searching blind.
